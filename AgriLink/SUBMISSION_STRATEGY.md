## 📋 Submission Form Content

### Primary Problem Statement: **Omni_AgriTech_3**

### Problem Statement (100-300 words)

Indian farmers face severe information asymmetry in agricultural markets. 60% of farmers don't have access to real-time mandi (market) prices, leading to exploitation by middlemen who reduce farmer income by 20-30%. Without price transparency, farmers cannot make informed selling decisions and often sell at unfavorable rates.

The lack of historical price trends prevents farmers from strategic timing of their produce sales. Information is fragmented across different states, districts, and commodities with no centralized platform. Farmers have no mechanism for price alerts when favorable market conditions arise.

This information gap causes farmers to lose approximately ₹50,000 Crores annually. Traditional methods of checking prices involve physically visiting multiple mandis or relying on word-of-mouth, which is time-consuming and often inaccurate. Rural farmers with limited internet access struggle even more.

The problem is exacerbated by language barriers, as most available platforms are English-only, while 85% of farmers prefer regional languages. Additionally, existing solutions don't provide comparative analysis across nearby markets or predictive insights based on historical data.

There's an urgent need for a comprehensive, real-time, multi-lingual mandi rates platform that integrates official government data sources and provides intelligent market insights to empower farmers with information for better price discovery.

---

### Proposed Solution (100-300 words)

AgriLink is a comprehensive web platform providing real-time mandi rates with advanced market intelligence, powered by official government APIs (data.gov.in and eNAM).

**Core Features:**

**1. Real-Time Price Data**
- Integration with data.gov.in and eNAM APIs for official, verified market prices
- Auto-refresh every 5 minutes ensuring latest information
- Coverage of 500+ mandis across all Indian states
- 1000+ commodities tracked daily with min, max, and modal prices

**2. Price Intelligence**
- 7-day price trend visualization with interactive sparkline charts
- Yesterday vs today comparison with percentage changes
- Price alert system notifying users of significant movements (>10%)
- Predictive analytics based on historical data patterns

**3. Smart Filtering & Search**
- State-wise and district-level filtering for localized information
- Category-based quick filters (Cereals, Vegetables, Fruits, Pulses, Spices)
- Commodity search across 102 crops
- Nearby market locator using GPS technology

**4. Accessibility Features**
- Multi-language support (English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada)
- Progressive Web App working offline with cached data
- Mobile-first responsive design optimized for low-bandwidth
- Touch-optimized interface for ease of use

**5. Bonus Features**
Beyond mandi rates, AgriLink includes 19 additional AgriTech solutions: AI crop diagnosis, smart irrigation, weather alerts, storage monitoring, farmer credit scoring, livestock health tracking, organic traceability, pest alerts, food surplus redistribution, bee colony monitoring, fishing zones, machinery sharing, and more - providing 100% coverage of all 20 OMNIKON AgriTech problem statements.

**Technology:** Built with React + TypeScript for reliability, deployed on scalable cloud infrastructure, with real-time API integrations ensuring data accuracy and freshness.

**Impact:** Targets 10,000+ farmers in Phase 1, with potential to increase farmer income by 25% through better price discovery and market transparency.
